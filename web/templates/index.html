CrossGen

Cross-domain analogical reasoning engine. Finds solutions to your problems from unexpected fields.

How It Works

1-2

Decompose & Abstract

Your problem is broken into functions, constraints, and contradictions. Four parallel lenses (SAPPhIRE, Biomimicry, WordTree, TRIZ) create domain-neutral abstractions.

3-4

Expand & Mine

Abstractions surface 6-8 candidate domains from mycology to acoustics. Each domain is mined for structural analogies with element-by-element mapping.

5-6

Synthesize & Evaluate

Cross-domain mechanisms become concrete solutions with candidate inferences — non-obvious predictions from the source domain. Scored on novelty, feasibility, and depth.

What Makes This Different

TRIZ

Contradiction matrix is a deterministic lookup — no hallucinated principles.

4 LENSES

Four parallel abstraction views reveal different cross-domain connections.

GENTNER

Relational mapping enforcement — rejects surface-level analogies like "both involve networks."

SAPPhIRE

Abstraction hierarchy as novelty dial — Effect level = high novelty, Parts level = low.
